Robert started on the tools in South Australia in the mid-1980s and has spent four decades building, supervising and repairing homes across Adelaide. That's the reason the reports read the way they do: he's fixed most of what he finds.

He inspects every property himself. There's no roster of subcontractors and no franchise office between you and the person who walked the roof void. If something in the report isn't clear, the number you ring is his.

Inspections are carried out to Australian Standards AS 4349.1 for building and AS 4349.3 for timber pests, and Bezemer Home & Pest Inspections carries current professional indemnity and public liability insurance.
