Two of the three major defects are moisture related and connected: stormwater is discharging against the south footing, and the salt damp above it is the result. Fixed in the right order, that's one trade, not two. The termite workings in the subfloor are inactive, but the conditions that attracted them are the same conditions. None of this makes the house a poor buy. It makes it a house with a drainage problem you should price before you sign.

Major defects

Each one photographed, located, and described in the words you'd use to get a quote.

PHOTO 4.2 · SOUTH ELEVATION

REF 4.2 · EXTERNAL WALLS

Salt damp to south wallMAJOR

WHERE
South elevation, between the laundry window and the south-east corner.
WHAT I FOUND
Rising moisture to 900mm above ground level. Render blown across 3.4 metres, mortar friable to finger pressure, salt crystallisation on the brick face.
WHY IT MATTERS
The damp proof course is either breached or was never installed at this section. Left alone it keeps climbing and takes the render and the mortar with it.
WHAT NEXT
Quote from a licensed salt damp contractor before settlement. Fix the stormwater at 4.3 first or you will be paying for this twice.
PHOTO 4.3 · NE CORNER

REF 4.3 · SITE DRAINAGE

Stormwater discharging to footingMAJOR

WHERE
North-east corner, downpipe from the rear gutter run.
WHAT I FOUND
Downpipe unconnected to the stormwater line, discharging directly onto soil hard against the footing. Active scour channel, approximately 120mm deep.
WHY IT MATTERS
Every storm sends roof water into the ground beside the footing. It's the cheapest defect in this report and the cause of the most expensive one.
WHAT NEXT
Connect to the existing stormwater line and reinstate the fall away from the building. Half a day for a plumber.
PHOTO 5.1 · SUBFLOOR

REF 5.1 · TIMBER PESTS (AS 4349.3)

Termite workings, subfloorMAJOR

WHERE
Subfloor, bearer above pier 4, mid-span on the southern run.
WHAT I FOUND
Mud leads to the bearer, no live termites present at the time of inspection. Timber sounds hollow across roughly 400mm. Subfloor ventilation restricted on the south side.
WHY IT MATTERS
Inactive today is not the same as safe. The damp, poorly ventilated subfloor is exactly what brought them in, and it hasn't changed.
WHAT NEXT
Timber pest management plan from a licensed pest controller, plus additional subfloor vents. Structural assessment of the bearer if the hollow section extends further than sounded.
